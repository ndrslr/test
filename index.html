Research interests

Broadly, I'm interested in the non-perturbative structure of string theory and quantum gravity, and in the discrete data such as symmetries, topological charges, and defect groups that low-energy effective field theory alone doesn't see. I'm currently working on two threads in Prof. Heckman's group.

Bordisms, TQFTs & the Swampland

I study how bordism groups constrain the non-perturbative structure of string/M-theory, in the spirit of the Swampland Cobordism Conjecture: global symmetries associated with duality bundles should be broken by new, singular objects, and bordism groups predict what those objects must be. Specifically, I'm computing Pin± and Spin bordism groups twisted by duality bundles (extending the reflection-brane analysis of 'Pintopia' beyond its orignal dimension/duality group) to identify the new branes needed to cancel candiddate discrete glonal symmetries of M-Theory and Type IIB string theory.

Generalized symmetries in SCFTs

I'm also working on generalized (higher-form and non-invertible) symmetries of superconformal field theories engineered via string/M-theory — using branes wrapped on cycles of the internal geometry, and Symmetry TFTs, to extract the defect group and global structure of a given SCFT. In particular, I'm looking at M-theory compactified on non-compact, not-necessarily-Calabi-Yau six-manifolds, to explore whether the SymTFT construction extends to give stable non-supersymmetric 4d SCFTs.
